for the bounded iteration in [portable review](portable-review.md), with per-ID limits.

Baseline diagnostics were inspected via own-asset family sheets and actual supplied
images/crops; missing standalone views are honestly marked as contextual only.
Selectors follow association > per-file manifest > matching collection/root.
This report is not another association/manifest or executable style framework.

Current selected source/export/image hashes and costs: [baseline](baseline.json),
[profiles](profiles.json), [resources](resources.json). Current matched static building
evidence: [first batch](first-batch.md) and [forest/units](forest-units.md). All 18 selected
source pairs and 27 portable pairs exist; four emission/form states and 60 actual
1× recordings retain software/halo/starter-motion limits. [Fidelity correction](export-fidelity.md)
closes exactly six proven palette-loss IDs; 24 affected-appearance clips were refreshed,
not all 60. Old wrong exports/captures remain explicitly historical. 78 other IDs
remain unchanged, with their assessed opportunities below; no all-105 improvement claim.
