## Named forest prototype hypotheses and observed results

- **D02/D04, blue/purple mushrooms:** regular upright caps and luminous stem
  hairlines competed with broad painted bodies. Tilted existing caps, undersides
  and spots coherently; removed the duplicate hairline meshes while retaining
  mapped stems, selective smoothing, pivots and palette. In the dominant
  [glowing mushrooms](evidence/hex_glowing_mushrooms-480.jpg) and
  [bioluminescent grove](evidence/hex_bioluminescent_grove-480.jpg), tilted rims
  are visibly less regular at 480/192; stem bodies are quieter at close range.
  This is restrained variation, not a new mushroom silhouette family. Existing
  smooth caps remain an authored adaptation of the more faceted forest reference.
- **D06, stone-bound glyph:** the original glyph occupied nearly the full tapered
  monolith face. Reduced it around its existing center without changing stone
  topology/maps. [Rune shrine](evidence/hex_rune_shrine-480.jpg) and
  [crystal grove](evidence/hex_crystal_grove-480.jpg) retain visibly wider stone
  margins. The smaller internal mark is not a 96-pixel recognition improvement.
  Floating shrine glyphs/circles were deliberately not shrunk. No emission-power
  or compositor change; portable bloom-off/on judgment remains open.
- **D06, foam contact:** complete floating bubble circles gave the channel a
  decorative overlay. Opened/elongated them into contact-facing crescents.
  Both [woodland bridge](evidence/hex_woodland_bridge-480.jpg) and
  [lantern bridge](evidence/hex_lantern_bridge-480.jpg) show the clearer broken
  downstream flow accents at 480/192. Falls/channel/endpoints remain unchanged;
  this does not certify animated water or an optical water shader.
- **D01/D05, crystal grove composition:** leaned supporting crystals and moved
  peripheral leaves toward existing contacts. The main vertical crystal remains
  dominant; [480](evidence/hex_crystal_grove-480.jpg) shows more directional
  secondary facets and slightly more open ground. Do not credit the unchanged
  crystal prototype or palette with a new material improvement.

Supporting cap changes in [mystical grove](evidence/hex_mystical_grove-480.jpg)
and [crystal shrine](evidence/hex_crystal_shrine-480.jpg) are incidental at 192,
not improvements to their unchanged focal crystal/altar mass. Their large planar
stone/crystal bodies and openings remain. At 96, most supporting mushroom
surface changes cannot be reliably distinguished. These dependents were exported
for coherent library freshness, not to inflate a visual-improvement tally.

## Units and equipment

- **Knight / D01:** [480](evidence/knight-480.jpg) shows a shorter, broader existing
  plume instead of the tall narrow top-heavy spike. Helmet/face, sword/shield and
  faction colors are retained. The changed contour remains readable in the
  provisional small views; it is not a new animation or armor-normal treatment.
- **Evil ranged / D01/D03/D04:** [current 480](evidence/evil_ranged_unit-480.jpg)
  and [192](evidence/evil_ranged_unit-192.jpg) show a compact directional hood,
  red neck accent and ivory loaded-bolt feathers. Iteration 1's taller wizard-like
  peak was rejected; its [original after view](evidence/iteration1/evil_ranged_unit.png)
  is retained, not silently relabeled as the accepted current source. The current
  hood is still more upright/faceted than the supplied E3 cloth hood. Red neck
  separation is visible at 192; feather detail is primarily a 480-level cue and
  no 96-pixel feather gain is claimed. Skull/eyes remain dominant.
- **Crossbow closure:** the standalone prop and equipped copy receive the same
  two grip-local feathers. Current GLB cost is +24 unique triangles in each copy;
  the final corrected unit adds 2,704 bytes, no material/image definitions versus
  original baseline (the pre-fix candidate had one extra material/2,900 bytes). Original stock,
  string/limbs, grip pivot, attachment scale and rig interfaces are unchanged.
  Later [portable review](portable-review.md) adds standalone paired framing and
  six actual 1× equipped clips. Observed attachment continuity is retained; generic
  crossbow starter aim is not a new reload/projectile/animation refinement.

All eight units retain the shared clips and socket interfaces. Mechanical
