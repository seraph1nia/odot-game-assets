What transfers — and what does not

Roots are structure, not a border

Trunks become arches; roots grip foundations; moss shelves follow overlaps; vines travel along aged joints. Increase layered organic coverage markedly while keeping entrances, machinery and role signs readable. Intrinsic growth must travel inside the building export, not disappear with presentation terrain.

Trade first, decoration second

Wheel/hoist/log racks, bread/cap shelter, low gold portal/cart, standing cut blocks/crane, tall dark metal outcrop/rails, flowing loom cloth, peaked market tents/scales, branched civic crown/glazing. These are different massing rhythms, not eight copies of one cottage.

Quiet bodies, small lights

Cool gray-green vegetation, weathered browns and slate blues support small warm interiors and narrow cyan/violet accents. Material grading must survive neutral exported views. Dimming lights alone is not a style overhaul; crushing black recesses loses tabletop readability.

Illustration is not attained 3D fidelity

Atmospheric forest layers, painterly edges and tiny illustrated flowers do not imply those details already exist in our assets. Keep broad planes and selective organic softness. Fine lichen/grain/wear belongs in portable maps; geometry handles silhouettes, roots and functional layers.

Existing actual outputs: the gap

Woodcutter: a tidy log cottage

Bright grass, clean straight timber, a small roof shrub and compact log cue. W0's lumbermill has a tree-led industrial gesture: wheel, hanging hoist and long timber stacks. Adapt its working silhouette; do not hide the axe/door beneath generic ivy.

BEFORE · saved presentation render, not normalized

Weaver: loom identity, generic roof mass

The loom, bolts and cloth already supply a useful role cue. The roof/cottage is still close to many other workshops, with saturated blue/green support. Exaggerate draped cloth and an asymmetric working canopy; layer vines/moss at supports while preserving the open loom and sign.

BEFORE · older saved studio

Knight: readable chibi, little travel story

Helmet/plume/shield give clear identity and must survive. The woodland adaptation should strengthen proud shield/plume proportions and travel-worn layers without realistic anatomy or foliage across joints. W0 contains no knight image, so this is authored adaptation, not reference fidelity.

BEFORE · current forest presentation

Mushroom grove: focal caps, tidy ground

Distinct caps and crystals already read clearly. Overgrowth should connect stems, rocks and roots rather than obscure caps or uniformly ring the hex. Mute bodies, reserve emission for gills/runes and narrow highlights, and preserve footprint/endpoints. No building ornament belongs on static fauna or flat occupation floors.

These baseline views have differing old studio settings. They establish observed shape/detail gaps, not a quantitative palette comparison. The actual rollout needs matched before/after exported views at provisional 96/192/480 inspection tiers and source/export/check hash binding.

Scope decision: native UI

The catalog also contains 15 native UI entries. The supplied board has no native UI design; the existing Ledger suite already has a muted woodland theme, original seal and source-bound native captures. A 3D skill cannot silently authorize a UI redesign or count those rows as restyled.

Options for firstmate: explicitly retain the native suite as a category-appropriate exception, or authorize an API-compatible native UI style pass and renewed standalone native evidence. Neither option involves the game repository or game tests.

Preservation and honest gates

  • Exact before sources/exports and previews are preserved locally before any authoring. The baseline binds hashes and actual export selectors.
  • Retain IDs, origins, authored grip scale, shared 19-bone skeleton, 24 FPS and six clips. Preserve flat hex footprint, water endpoints and all flat-ground/overlay interfaces.
  • Four decal textures, packed texture companions and root overview scenes are dependencies to synchronize or explicitly disclose, not silently ignore.
  • Mechanical tests, headless CPU image evidence and artist/game-camera acceptance are different claims. No game launch, installation, import or global-setting changes are authorized.
