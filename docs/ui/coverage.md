## Still unaccepted, not waived

E–G tasks remain in progress for broader projection/composition acceptance. The
checked package is suitable for coordinated delivery review of **this boundary**;
it does not satisfy these remaining criteria by relabeling fixtures:

- Exhaustive HUD phase × selected-context × eligibility combinations; lobby/start,
  fallen/stale-city, connecting/reconnecting/rejected session feedback and one/four-
  city edge-case input. Current HUD covers representative selections/states, not
  every cross product. All constructor tabs/role selectors are populated, but not
  every row has actual activation coverage (unavailable Mage intentionally disabled).
- Details currently composes a forecast, last-clear reward, field roster and queued
  allocation sample; it does not switch its expanded body between paid-current-wave,
  last-wave/fresh-session projections. The HUD receipt is checked independently.
- Town hall is an occupied nine-reserve representative body. Empty/stale hall,
  complete storage-upgrade/occupied-sale rejection flows, roster full/empty/fragmented
  tiles and recovery-eligibility combinations need additional fixture acceptance.
  Game generation/lifetime/atomic transfer validation belongs to future adapters.
- Model preview loading/picking/projection, world health bar offscreen behavior and
  moving label-removal compatibility are deliberately not implemented here. Native
  `Body/PreviewSlot` and health/status samples establish presentation seams only.
- Real async completion after hide/reopen or stale friend/settings/session callbacks,
  invite-join/host-leave lifecycle, account/platform/update/persistence failures.
  These require game services; mock text/busy projection checks do not certify them.
- Dialog visual acceptance at 1600×900/1920×1080, extreme small windows, localization/
  RTL, HiDPI, game canvas-items/expand, older engine/native desktop/controller/mobile.
  Current measured large sizes apply to HUD/start menu, not every dialog/state.

Native scroll ownership is decided when `WatchUI.scroll()` is constructed. Host
components before their `_ready()`/body construction under the intended scroll
owner; reparenting an initialized standalone list into a modal is not certified.
Full identities of very long duplicate friends remain available through tooltips,
not guaranteed inline; keyboard-only tooltip discoverability remains a later UX gate.
