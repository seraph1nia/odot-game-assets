# D07 — Thematic and family consistency

## Evidence and observation

[Supplied sets](../references/index.md) share medieval timber/stone/leaf and
miniature construction while changing signature objects. E-series skulls,
spikes/horns, dark cloth and red signals are a faction override, not a new
photoreal rendering style. The dreaming-forest expansion is present authored
content but has no explicitly associated supplied picture.

## Standard and rationale

Inherit the common rules through family routes; add only named role/faction/
composition exceptions with evidence and reason. Reuse established village,
forest and enchanted libraries for repeated forms. New motifs are documented
as authored adaptations, never supplied-reference observations.
Shared rules prevent each task from inventing a separate mini style guide.

## Scope and exceptions

Current buildings, units/equipment, props/terrain and forest families. No detailed
UI/audio/VFX standard is inferred from empty category folders. Dreaming trees,
fauna and wing markings inherit common treatment as an authored adaptation;
no reference-fidelity score applies without an explicit supplied association.

## Qualitative examples

- **Pass anchor:** E3's crossbow and A3's axes share chunky construction despite
  different faction/body treatment; B2's telescope still belongs to the timber/
  stone village instead of modern industrial hardware.
- **Fail criterion:** scanned realistic rocks next to simplified toy joinery,
  modern props included merely because they are low-poly, or generated dream
  previews cited as untouched supplied art.
- Compare a cross-family lineup under the same profile when such work is authorized.

## Uncertainty and calibration

A single picture does not settle every future thematic extension or license.
Consult [reference authority](../references/index.md#authority) for unsupported
motifs. Do not acquire external references to fill a gap without scope/rights
approval. Existing prototype/code ownership remains in
[the library procedure](../blender/libraries.md), not a new guide-owned library.

Family routes: [buildings](../families/buildings.md), [units](../families/units.md),
[environment/dreaming](../families/environment.md), [props](../families/props.md).
